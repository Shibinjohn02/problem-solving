# Think Heap when:

“Top K”
“Smallest / Largest repeatedly”
Streaming data
Need quick min/max access
Scheduling problems


# Heap ka kaam hi hota hai:

“Mujhe repeatedly largest element efficiently do.”
“Main pura order maintain nahi karunga… bas largest quickly dunga.”


# Heap: sirf best/top element maintain karta hai

Isi wajah se:
insert → O(log n)
remove top → O(log n)
peek top → O(1)

# Heap ko aise socho:
“VIP queue”

Sab log sorted nahi hain.

Bas:
sabse important banda front pe hona chahiye.

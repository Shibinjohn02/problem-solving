
# Requirements Gathering

## Extensible

1. Kya future me naye vehicle types add ho sakte hain?
   - Car, Bike, Truck, SUV etc.

2. Kya multiple payment methods support karne hain?
   - UPI, Card, Cash etc.

3. Kya pricing strategy future me change ho sakti hai?
   - Hourly, Daily, Weekend pricing


## Dynamic

1. Kya vehicles dynamically available/unavailable honge?

2. Kya users advance booking kar sakte hain?

3. Kya system multiple rental locations support karega?


## Concurrency

1. Kya same vehicle ko same time pe multiple users book kar sakte hain?

2. Kya booking ke time locking needed hai to avoid double booking?

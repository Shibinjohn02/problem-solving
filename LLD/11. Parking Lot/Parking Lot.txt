
Flow:
User → Enter → Collect Ticket → Park Vehicle → Payment → Exit

- Vehicle
- Car
- Bike
- Truck

- ParkingSpot
- ParkingLot
- Ticket

- Payment
- PaymentStrategy
- FareStrategy

- EntryGate
- ExitGate
- ParkingManager

Step 2: Add Responsibilities:

Vehicle:
(no responsibilities needed)

ParkingSpot:
- parkVehicle()
- removeVehicle()
- isAvailable()

ParkingLot:
- addParkingSpot()
- getParkingSpots()

EntryGate:
- generateTicket()

ExitGate:
- processExit()

ParkingManager:
- parkVehicle(vehicle)
- removeVehicle(ticket)
- findAvailableSpot()

Payment:
- processPayment()

PaymentStrategy:
- pay(amount)

FareStrategy:
- calculateFare(ticket)


Step 3: Connect them: How objects are related to each other:

ParkingLot HAS-A list/array of ParkingSpots
ParkingLot HAS-A EntryGate
ParkingLot HAS-A ExitGate
ParkingLot HAS-A ParkingManager
Payment HAS-A PaymentStrategy

ParkingManager HAS-A ParkingLot
ParkingManager accesses ParkingSpots
(via ParkingLot or directly)

Car IS-A Vehicle
Bike IS-A Vehicle
Truck IS-A Vehicle

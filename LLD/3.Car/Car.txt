
Step 1: Identify Entities:

- Car
- Engine
- Wheels

Note: There could many others entities like speaker etc

Step 2: Add Responsibilities:

Car:
- start()
- stop()
- drive()
- accelerate()

Engine:
- on()
- off()

Wheels:
- rotate()
- stop()

Step 3: Connect them:

- HAS-A: 
Car HAS-A Engine
Car HAS-A List of Wheels

- IS-A: Not needed here

Step 4: Add Features/Attributes:

Attributes:

Car:
- name
- color
- price
- carNumber

Engine:
- type
- horsepower

Wheels:
- size
- grip
